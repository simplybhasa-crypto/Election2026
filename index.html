Start with three steps

Ready when you are.

Election rules, registration deadlines, and voting options vary by state and territory. Use official sources to check what applies where you live.

  1. Check your registration

    Confirm your status and find your state's registration information at Vote.gov.

  2. Learn what's on your ballot

    Look up candidates and ballot measures using your state or local election office.

  3. Choose how you'll vote

    Check your polling place, early voting dates, mail ballot rules, and any ID requirements with your election office.
